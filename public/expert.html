Answering well

  • Use short, simple sentences. Many mothers read in a second language.
  • Say clearly if and when she should visit a clinic.
  • Don't prescribe medicines or doses. Refer her to a clinic instead.
  • Only take a question you can answer now. Unanswered questions are refunded after 2 hours.

If it sounds urgent

Bleeding, severe headache, blurred vision, fits, fever or the baby moving less: tell her to go to the nearest hospital now, in your first sentence.
