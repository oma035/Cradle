🚨 Danger signs come first

Bleeding, severe headache, fits, or your baby moving less? Cradle won't try to answer. It tells you to go to a hospital immediately.

💸 Refunds built in

Your payment is held in escrow. If no midwife answers in time, the money goes straight back to your wallet.

Payments use the x402 protocol on the Stellar network, so every one can be checked publicly. Nothing is hidden.
