Terms and conditions

Draft for the hackathon MVP. Have a lawyer review this before a public launch.

For everyone

Cradle provides general maternal-health information. It is not a diagnosis, prescription or emergency service, and it does not replace in-person care. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital.

For mothers

Cradle creates a Stellar wallet for you and keeps its key encrypted on your behalf. A paid midwife review is only charged after you tap Approve. Payments are held in escrow and refunded automatically if no expert answers within the stated time. Blockchain payments can't be reversed except through that refund.

For experts

You confirm that your qualifications are genuine and current, and that you will answer within your scope of practice. You will advise in-person care whenever a question needs it. You will keep mothers' questions confidential. Payouts are sent in USDC to the Stellar address you provide. You are responsible for any taxes on them. Cradle may suspend experts whose answers are unsafe.

Privacy

Questions are stored to deliver answers and improve safety. We don't sell personal data. Only approved experts see questions, and they don't see the mother's name.
