Common questions

Is Cradle a replacement for my clinic?

No. Cradle gives general guidance and connects you with verified midwives. It does not diagnose or prescribe. Keep going to your antenatal visits.

What does a midwife review cost?

$0.20 in USDC, paid from your Cradle wallet only after you tap Approve. If no midwife answers in time, it's refunded automatically.

How do I add money to my Cradle wallet?

Send /create to the bot to get your wallet address, then send USDC on the Stellar network to it from an exchange or a wallet app. Cradle covers the network fees.

Who are the experts?

Midwives, nurses and doctors who apply with their certification. Every application is checked by hand before approval.

What happens in an emergency?

If you mention a danger sign such as bleeding, a severe headache or your baby moving less, Cradle tells you to go to a hospital right away and doesn't try to answer.

In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital.
